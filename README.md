# 🌞 Build Your Perfect Day

A cozy little browser game where you plan out your perfect day by picking activities within a limited energy budget — then get a personalized mini diary entry written about it, plus a score and a grade for how your day went.

Built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no frameworks, no build tools, no dependencies. Just open the page and play.

## 🎮 How It Works

1. You start with 10 energy points for the day.
2. Choose from 12 activities (Sleep In, Morning Coffee, Sunrise Jog, Work on a Hobby, Dance Party, and more), each with its own energy cost and happiness points.
3. Try to fill your day without going over budget.
4. Certain activity pairs unlock bonus combos for extra points:
   - ☕ + 🏃 Coffee + Jog → Perfect Morning Combo
   - 🍝 + 🎬 Cook + Movie → Cozy Night In
   - 📞 + 💃 Call a Friend + Dance → Social Butterfly
   - 📖 + 🌌 Book + Stargazing → Peaceful Soul
5. Click Plan My Day to see your final score, grade, and a diary entry generated from the activities you picked.
6. Hit play again to try a different combination and chase a higher score.

## ✨ Features

- Live energy bar that updates as you check/uncheck activities
- Friendly error messages if you pick nothing or go over budget
- Bonus combo detection for smart activity pairings
- Auto-generated diary entry based on your specific choices
- Score-based grading, from "An Okay Day" to "A Truly Perfect Day!"
- Warm, hand-drawn visual style (Kalam font, soft sunbeam background)
- Fully responsive, single-page, no backend required

## 🚀 Running It Locally

No installation or server needed:

1. Download or clone this repo.
2. Make sure `index.html` and `style.css` are in the same folder.
3. Open `index.html` in any modern web browser.

That's it — the game runs entirely client-side.
